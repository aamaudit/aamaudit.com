Stay Informed

UAE Regulatory Updates

Key changes affecting UAE businesses right now. Our team actively advises clients on all of the below.

Effective June 2023

UAE Corporate Tax — 9%

UAE Corporate Tax at 9% now applies to most mainland and free zone businesses with taxable income above AED 375,000. Businesses must register with the FTA, maintain IFRS-compliant accounts, and file annual returns. Most companies now require audited financial statements as a result.

Applies to financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023
Annual Filing Required

Economic Substance Regulations (ESR)

UAE companies conducting Relevant Activities — including holding, IP, distribution, headquarters, and financial services — must file annual ESR notifications and reports demonstrating genuine economic activity in the UAE. Penalties for non-compliance are significant.

Annual notification and report required for in-scope entities
Ongoing Compliance

VAT & Excise Tax Obligations

UAE VAT at 5% remains a core compliance requirement, with quarterly FTA filings, recoverability rules, and voluntary disclosure obligations. Excise tax applies to specific goods including tobacco, energy drinks, and carbonated beverages.

Quarterly VAT returns required for registered businesses
