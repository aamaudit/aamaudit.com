Our Story

Four Decades of Financial Integrity

Ali Al Mohairy Auditing was established in Abu Dhabi in 1984 by Mr. Ali Sultan Almheiri with a singular mission: to provide UAE businesses with the financial clarity and compliance assurance they need to grow with confidence.

"Auditing UAE businesses with independence and rigor since 1984. Our legacy is built on one thing — the trust our clients place in us."

Over four decades, our firm has grown from a single Abu Dhabi office to a multi-city operation with offices in Dubai and Sharjah, serving more than 250 active clients across a wide range of industries. Our accounts are accepted by the Central Bank of the UAE, all major domestic banks, and international financial institutions.

Our success is rooted in our people. Our professional staff undergo continuous international training, workshops, and seminars to stay ahead of evolving regulations — from IFRS changes to new UAE Corporate Tax rules — ensuring every client receives advice that is both technically accurate and commercially relevant.

1984
Founded in Abu Dhabi, UAE
  • 1984 — Founded by Mr. Ali Sultan Almheiri in Abu Dhabi with a vision of financial excellence.
  • 1990s — Expanded into tax consultancy, VAT advisory, and management consulting services.
  • 2000s — Opened offices in Dubai and Sharjah. Grew to 250+ active clients across the UAE.

  • Today — Ministry of Economy registered. Accounts accepted by Central Bank of UAE and international banks.
Our Process

How We Work

A clear, structured process that keeps you informed and in control at every stage of the engagement.

01
Initial Consultation

We meet to understand your business, goals, regulatory obligations, and specific audit or advisory requirements — at no charge.

02
Engagement Planning

We define the scope, agree on fees, issue an engagement letter, and assign the right team members for your industry and size.

03
Fieldwork & Audit

Our team conducts a thorough on-site or remote examination of your financial records, controls, and supporting documentation.

04
Review & Reporting

We prepare clear, actionable findings, issue the signed audit report, and walk you through any recommendations in plain language.

05
Ongoing Support

We remain available year-round for follow-up questions, regulatory filings, and advisory — not just during audit season.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Three principles that have guided every engagement for over 40 years.

Integrity

We uphold the highest ethical standards in every engagement. Our clients trust us with their most sensitive financial information, and we honor that trust unconditionally. Our independence is non-negotiable.

Precision

Financial decisions depend on accurate data. We bring meticulous attention to detail to every audit and advisory engagement, ensuring our findings are thorough, reliable, and defensible.

Our Credentials

Why Clients Choose Us

The experience, recognition, and technical depth that set us apart in the UAE market.

Ministry of Economy Registered

We are a licensed auditing firm registered with the UAE Ministry of Economy. Our signed audit reports carry the legal standing required for regulatory submissions, bank financing, and free zone license renewals.

International Standards

We conduct all audits in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (ISA) and prepare financial statements under IFRS — ensuring global-level quality recognized by international banks and investors.

Continuously Trained Team

Our qualified professionals receive continuous international training covering UAE Corporate Tax, ESR, VAT, and evolving IFRS standards — so your business always has access to current, accurate advice.

Proven Track Record

More than 250 active clients across 8 industries, sustained over four decades. Our retention rate reflects the consistent, reliable delivery that UAE businesses — from SMEs to large corporates — depend on year after year.
