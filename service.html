Regulatory Compliance

UAE-Specific Compliance Services

Beyond traditional audit, we help UAE businesses navigate the regulatory obligations that apply to virtually every company operating in the Emirates today.

UAE Corporate Tax (9%)

We handle FTA registration, taxable income calculation, Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) status, and annual return preparation — keeping you compliant with the UAE Corporate Tax regime while minimising your effective tax burden within the law.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML/CFT) Compliance

Registration with the goAML system, and assistance establishing the reviewing, reporting, and documentation requirements your business needs to stay compliant.

UBO Registration & Maintenance

Ultimate Beneficial Owner register setup, timely updates, and compliant record maintenance — keeping your business aligned with UAE beneficial ownership requirements.

Free Zone Authority Requirements

Annual audit and financial reporting submissions for DIFC, ADGM, JAFZA, DMCC, RAKEZ, and other free zones — we manage submissions directly on your behalf.
